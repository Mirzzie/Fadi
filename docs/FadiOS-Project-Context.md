# Fadi product context

**Status:** Current product doctrine

**Updated:** 23 September 2026

Fadi is a personal career operating system with an agent that coordinates the whole journey. It helps a person understand their record, choose a direction, find opportunities, prepare, apply, learn from outcomes, and turn completed work into credible public proof.

Fadi is not a collection of AI forms and it is not a chat window attached to a dashboard. The UI, voice session, tools, work plan, career record, documents, portfolio, labs, and approvals are surfaces of one agent working toward the person's active goal.

## The promise

Fadi should answer four questions without making the person understand its internal modules:

1. What am I trying to achieve?
2. What should I do next?
3. What did Fadi do, and why?
4. What needs my decision?

The durable lifecycle is:

> Choose a goal → find an opportunity or gap → prepare only where needed → prove the work → apply or share → learn from the outcome → choose the next action.

Apply and Prepare are phases inside this lifecycle. They may change emphasis, but they must never hide data or disconnect features.

## Product rules

### One truthful career record

Imported résumés, LinkedIn exports, completed projects, certifications, courses, work history, and manual entries are sources. They do not become independent competing truths. Fadi preserves sources, extracts candidate records, asks when identity is uncertain, and maintains one canonical career record with history and provenance.

Different wording for a DevOps role and an IT-support role is a rendering of the same underlying fact. Direction and job selections decide relevance; they do not clone the experience.

### Projections do not own facts

Résumés, cover letters, emails, the portfolio, LinkedIn drafts, interview stories, and public profiles are projections of the canonical record. A projection may keep its wording, ordering, media, layout, and publication state. It must reference the source record and report when that source changes.

Portfolio media is optional proof attached to a career record or portfolio presentation. Missing media must not become a warning that implies the record is false.

### Fadi orchestrates; the person remains accountable

Fadi may recommend, draft, verify, and carry out authorised work. It must show consequential effects before acting, keep receipts afterward, and ask for approval at the boundary where the user would reasonably expect control. It must never fabricate a credential, employer, result, metric, or completed action.

### The next action beats the feature menu

Today chooses the smallest useful next step across opportunities, preparation, practice, documents, portfolio, and follow-up. Specialist pages exist for depth, but the person should not manually relay state among them.

### Completion creates one reviewed outcome

Finishing a lab, course, project, or external activity produces one completion package. The person verifies what happened once. From that approved outcome Fadi can propose:

- a career record;
- portfolio placement and optional proof media;
- résumé wording;
- an interview story;
- a public post or campaign.

These are linked projections, not five separate entry flows.

### Live voice means shared context

Voice and text use the same work item, conversation, tools, policy, and context envelope. Listening is visible and user-initiated. Fadi can remain context-aware without an always-open microphone. A useful live session supports natural turn-taking, interruption, a transcript, and clear action confirmation.

### Confidence must match evidence

Fadi distinguishes facts, inference, estimates, and unknowns. Scores require anchored rubrics. A failed identity match or unsupported claim becomes a question or deferral, never a confident guess.

## Information architecture

The product has four destinations:

- **Today** — goal, next action, recent agent work, decisions, and a small number of suggestions.
- **Opportunities** — roles, applications, and people.
- **Practice** — learning, projects/labs, and interview practice.
- **My career** — canonical record, documents, portfolio, and directions.

Profile and Settings belong to the account menu. Infrastructure such as machines, permissions, raw activity, and provider configuration appears in context or under advanced administration.

## Success criteria

Fadi succeeds when a person can:

- import or add work once and understand what Fadi accepted;
- change direction without duplicating history;
- start from Today and complete the next useful action;
- finish practical work and see every proposed downstream update together;
- speak to Fadi and continue the same task in text or UI;
- understand what Fadi knows, inferred, changed, and still needs;
- revoke access, correct a record, or inspect a consequential action.

Feature count, chat volume, generated text, streaks, and opaque numeric scores are not success measures.
