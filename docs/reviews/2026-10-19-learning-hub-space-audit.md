# Learning Hub space and hierarchy audit — 19 October 2026

## Evidence and design basis

The visual system follows the REA observations already captured for Pipecat
(`ev_66687714758c19f7ecc2215ba7b5f66220818bebc5edf9f613ff259c66b7b4c3`) and Paperclip
(`ev_26ad3b66dfc011bdbba8a71bad9d302da51e45283409dc258d3aa43232b42ef4`): strong editorial
hierarchy, compact operational rows, thin rules, and progressive disclosure. The isolated REA
browser has no authenticated Fadi session, so runtime visual verification of this private page
remains a signed-in follow-up rather than an observed claim.

## Authoritative state

The existing `buildPracticeHome` result still decides what to continue, recommend, browse, and
classify as finished. Project, commitment, flagship, report, and simulation data continue to come
from their existing repositories and actions. The presentation creates no new learning status.

## Primary journey

The initial surface now answers, in order:

1. What was I doing?
2. Can I practise a real task now?
3. Which substantial projects are worth undertaking?

Secondary active work, skill gaps, finished work, machine/activity links, and the long-term plan
remain available through two native disclosure controls. This removes initial scanning cost without
deleting or duplicating capability.

## Visible changes

- Editorial Practice introduction tied to the active career direction.
- Flat, ruled continuation and simulation surfaces.
- The Customer Success simulation is promoted as an actual task, not a decorative experiment card.
- Secondary learning inventory is one compact disclosure with an item count.
- The longer blueprint is a separate disclosure.
- Removed colored icon tiles, rounded panel shells, and unnecessary internal whitespace.

## Primary actions

When work is active, **Continue** remains the authoritative primary action. When nothing is active,
the visible simulation and flagship choices provide honest starts; no artificial recommendation is
invented by the layout.
