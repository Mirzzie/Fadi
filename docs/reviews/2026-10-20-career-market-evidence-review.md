## Requirements for Fadi's evidence model

- **Vacancy provenance:** canonical employer URL and requisition ID; source type; observed, posted and last-verified dates; collection/query/filter version; status and duplicate grouping. Distinguish employer vacancy evidence from aggregator totals and macro reports.
- **Role accessibility:** stated experience range, mandatory versus preferred requirements, duties, location, working pattern, transport/licence requirements and evidence excerpt. Preserve unknowns.
- **Compensation:** published versus inferred amount, currency, period, range, contracted hours, qualifying components and source. Never substitute a salary-survey midpoint for an offer.
- **Permit assessment:** route considered; applicant qualification/date relevance; duties/classification evidence; employer support as confirmed yes, confirmed no, or unknown; contract duration; applicable source/effective date and unresolved conditions. A salary-only result is not eligibility.
- **Separate applicant timelines:** actual permission expiry; renewal eligibility and application status; conditional extension; qualification concession window. User-confirmed dates must not be overwritten by extracted guesses.
- **Portfolio proof:** claim, artefact/repository link, commercial/lab distinction, personal contribution, validation, limitations and verification status. Keep unverified numerical claims out of generated CV prose.
- **Decision reporting:** deduplicated observed sample size, explicit exclusions, missing-field coverage, role-family application outcomes and observation window. Label forecasts as forecasts and recommendations as judgments. Show insufficient evidence when the sample cannot sustain a comparison.
- **Next action:** surface the single most useful missing fact or proof task, with the source and reason available in details. This is an evidence-design recommendation, not a claim that a new collection connector or scheduled crawler is implemented.
